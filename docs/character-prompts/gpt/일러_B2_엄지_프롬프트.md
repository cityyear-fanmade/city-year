# 엄지(카포) 일러스트 시트 프롬프트 — 4.5등신, 일러 스타일, 몸·머리 분리

범위: `docs/art-direction.md` 0절. 엄지 기본 1벌(카포) + 최고 직급(대부) 선택 1벌, 평상 표정 1종 × 머리 모양 2종.
순서: ① 몸 시트 → 마음에 들면 ② 머리 시트. 도트(B2)와 같은 비율·같은 옷이어야 한다.
몸 시트에는 머리카락이 없어야 한다(머리는 시트에서 얹는다). 원래 캐릭터의 긴 머리가 어깨 뒤로 흐르는 부분은 몸에 그리지 않는다.

## ① 몸 시트 (머리 없음) — 먼저 보낸다
첨부: `_ref_dnf_illust_capo.png`(옷 정확히), 도트 B2 몸 시트(`_src_b2_body.png`, 비율·옷 참고).

```
Create a full-body character illustration sheet, BODY ONLY (no head, no hair), in a high-quality anime game-art style (Dungeon Fighter Online-level costume detail with a Maplestory-style expressive character feel). Clean varied-weight outlines, cel shading with soft partial shading, clear difference between metal, leather and cloth.

PROPORTIONS (important)
- About 4.5 heads tall stylized proportions: head clearly bigger than realistic, but NOT a tiny chibi. Slim torso, medium-length legs, hands and feet slightly large and readable. Same proportions as the attached pixel-art body sheet.
- Body height (feet to the top of the magenta head circle) is 1000 px.

HEAD ANCHOR (very important)
- NO head and NO hair anywhere on the body (no hair falling over the shoulders or back). Where the head would be, draw ONE solid pure magenta circle (#FF00FF), flat color, no outline, diameter 220 px, sitting on the neck. The shirt collar and the red tie are visible below it.

COSTUME (match the attached reference exactly, add nothing, remove nothing)
- Black pinstripe three-piece suit with vest, white shirt with sleeves rolled to the forearm on one side, red tie, gold chain across the vest, brown leather belt with a gold buckle, a thigh holster with two buckled leather straps, black gloves, polished brown lace-up boots with a small heel.
- Long dark-red overcoat with ornate gold trim and gold vine-like patterns on the hem, worn open and hanging from the shoulders, gold epaulette with hanging gold cords on the near shoulder.
- Palette: crimson, near-black, dark brown, gold, white.

TWO FIGURES on one sheet, side by side, same scale, same ground line, each standing full body (feet visible, nothing cropped), slightly turned 3/4 to the right:
1) CAPO (default): the pose is relaxed and authoritative: back straight, weight on one leg, one hand tidying the coat lapel at the chest, the other hand relaxed at the side.
2) DON (highest rank): same body and same pose family, but a richer version of the same outfit: a heavier, longer coat with more gold embroidery and a second gold epaulette cord, a gold brooch on the chest. Nothing unrelated to the reference.

BACKGROUND: perfectly flat pure green #00FF00, no ground shadow, no text, no labels, no borders.
```

## ② 머리 시트 (단발 · 숏컷, 평상) — 몸이 마음에 들면 보낸다
첨부: 도트 B2 머리 시트(`_src_b2_head.png`, 머리 모양 참고), ①의 몸 시트(크기 참고).

```
Create a head illustration sheet to be placed on the magenta circle of the attached body sheet, in the same style as the body (clean varied-weight outlines, cel shading with soft partial shading, expressive eyes).

COLOR RULES (the game recolors these, follow strictly):
- Hair: ONLY neutral gray shades (4-5 steps, dark to light highlight), no color tint at all. Gray, not white.
- Eyes: ONLY a saturated cyan (#00C8FF) iris with a dark pupil and a white highlight; nothing else on the head is cyan.
- Skin pale peach, a small gold drop earring on the visible ear.

TWO HAIR SHAPES (two figures, side by side):
A) BOB: chin-length bob, bangs falling over one eye.
B) SHORT: short messy cut with one tuft sticking up.

EXPRESSION: calm and arrogant (half-lidded confident eyes, a faint closed-mouth smirk), facing slightly to the right, 3/4 view. Head only, no neck, no shoulders. Each head is about 220 px tall so that it fits the 220 px magenta circle (same scale as the body sheet). Hair must NOT extend onto where the body is: keep all hair strands within the head silhouette.

BACKGROUND: perfectly flat pure green #00FF00, no text, labels or borders.
```

## 받은 뒤 확인
같은 옷인가(참고 이미지와 비교) · 도트 B2와 같은 비율·같은 옷인가 · 머리 자리에 마젠타 원이 있고 머리카락이 몸에 걸리지 않았나 · 머리 시트의 눈이 청록, 머리카락이 회색인가 · 합성했을 때 목과 옷깃이 맞는가.
