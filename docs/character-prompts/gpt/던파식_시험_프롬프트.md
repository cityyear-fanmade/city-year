# 던파식 시험: 현실 비율 일러스트 + 같은 캐릭터 도트 한 쌍

2026-11-08 시험 방향: 일러스트(상태창·외형 바꾸기)와 전투 도트를 나누고, 둘 다 **현실 비율(약 7등신)**로 간다.
이번 시험의 목적은 "같은 캐릭터로 보이는 한 쌍이 AI로 일관되게 나오는가"를 보는 것이다.
머리색·눈색 교체, 소속 옷 교체는 한 쌍이 성공한 뒤에 방법을 정한다(이번엔 하지 않는다).

캐릭터(시험용): 엄지 · 카포. 성인, 어두운 정장 차림, 과한 노출 없음. 붉은 머리, 금빛 눈.
순서: ① 일러스트 → 마음에 들면 ② 도트 시트(① 이미지를 첨부) → ③ 확인 항목으로 판정.

---

## ① 일러스트 (전신, 현실 비율)

첨부: 던파식으로 참고할 일러(고퀄 전신 · 지난번 보내준 것은 포즈·분위기 참고만), 기존 엄지 카포 치비 일러(옷 참고).

```
Create ONE high-quality full-body character illustration, semi-realistic anime game art (Dungeon Fighter Online-style character art): painterly cel shading, crisp clean line art, rich detailed costume, dramatic but clean lighting. Plain light gray background, no text, no border.

CHARACTER
- Adult woman or man is NOT specified: draw an androgynous adult (late 20s), tall and slim, 7.5 heads tall, realistic proportions (NOT chibi, NOT super-deformed).
- Hair: long-ish layered crimson-red hair, side bangs covering one eye slightly, one small gold drop earring.
- Eyes: amber gold.
- Outfit (a mafia "Capo" rank, modest and elegant, no exposed skin except face, neck and hands): black pinstripe three-piece suit with a vest, red tie, brown leather belt with a gold buckle, black gloves, polished brown boots with a small heel, and a long dark-red overcoat with ornate gold trim worn open, hanging off the shoulders, with a gold chain across the vest. Subtle gold epaulette on one shoulder.
- Pose: standing, weight on one leg, calm and dangerous expression, one hand relaxed at the side, the other lightly touching the coat lapel. Facing slightly to the right (3/4 view). Full body from head to boots, feet visible, nothing cropped.
- Keep the color palette to: crimson, near-black, dark brown, gold, off-white skin.

Output: one image, one character, standing, centered.
```

## ② 도트 시트 (①의 같은 캐릭터)

첨부: ①에서 고른 일러스트 1장(반드시). 전투용이라 머리까지 포함한 한 몸이다(이번 시험은 몸·머리 분리 안 함).

```
Using the attached illustration, create a pixel-art battle sprite sheet of EXACTLY THE SAME character, in the style of Dungeon Fighter Online sprites (detailed painterly pixel art, realistic proportions, soft shading made of clean pixel clusters).

STYLE
- True pixel art: hard pixel edges, NO blur, NO anti-aliasing, NO gradients. Hand-painted pixel shading with 3-4 tone steps per material. 1-pixel dark outline, thinner on the inside.
- The character is 144 pixels tall on a logical grid, scaled up by exactly 4x so each pixel is a crisp 4x4 block. Maximum 40 colors in the whole sheet.
- Realistic proportions (7.5 heads tall) exactly like the attached illustration. SAME face, SAME crimson hair shape and color, SAME outfit, SAME gold trim, SAME palette in every frame.
- Side view, the character faces RIGHT. Empty hands (no weapon). No effects, no motion lines, no shadows, no text.

BACKGROUND
- Perfectly flat pure green #00FF00, nothing else.

SHEET LAYOUT (5 rows, one pose per row, frames from left to right)
Every cell is the same size (200x200 logical pixels). The character's feet touch the same bottom line in every frame (except lying-down frames, which rest on that line). 8 pixels of empty margin between cells.
Row 1  IDLE (loop), 4 frames: slow breathing, coat hem and hair sway 1-2 pixels.
Row 2  ATTACK (melee strike), 5 frames: ready stance, wind-up leaning back, lunge with an extended punch, follow-through, recover.
Row 3  DODGE, 3 frames: start, leaning back with a step away and coat flaring, return.
Row 4  HIT, 3 frames: flinch with the head thrown back, strongest recoil, recover.
Row 5  DOWN, 4 frames: stagger, knees buckle, falling backwards, lying flat on the ground.

Keep the character's proportions, face, hair and outfit identical in all frames. No labels, borders, grid lines, numbers or watermarks.
```

## ③ 판정 (Claude가 받은 뒤 확인)
1. 같은 캐릭터인가: 얼굴·머리·옷 장식·색이 프레임 사이, 그리고 일러스트와 맞는가.
2. 프레임이 어긋나는가: 칸 크기, 발 위치, 키가 프레임마다 흔들리는가.
3. 도트 맛이 나는가: 뭉개진 선·그라데이션이 섞였는가(섞이면 색 수 줄이고 정수 배율로 다시 뽑는다).
4. 시험 무대에 올렸을 때 높이(PC 약 200px · 모바일 약 150px)에서 읽히는가.
5. 통과하면: 머리색·눈색·소속 옷 교체를 어떻게 할지(레이어 분리 vs 소속별 전체 시트) 다음 단계로 정한다.
