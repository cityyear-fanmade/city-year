# B2: 치비(2~3등신)와 던파(7.5등신) 사이 — 약 4.5등신

2026-11-08 결정: 던파식(7.5등신)은 접고, 이전 치비 느낌에서 **머리를 조금 줄이고 몸을 조금 늘린** 중간 비율로 간다.
일러스트는 "일러 스타일"(채색이 있는 그림체)을 유지하고, 도트도 이전 B보다 한 단계 정밀하게 간다.

| | 치비(지금) | **B2(이번)** | 던파식(접음) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 등신 | 약 2.5 | **약 4.5** | 7.5 |
| 도트 높이 | 80칸 | **100칸** | 144칸 |
| 마젠타 원 지름 | 24칸(30%) | **22칸(22%)** | (분리 안 함) |
| 색 수 | 20색 | **28색 이하** | 40색 |
| 머리·색 교체 | 됨 | **됨(몸·머리 분리 유지)** | 안 됨 |

공통: 배경 순수 초록(#00FF00) · 오른쪽을 바라봄 · 도트가 또렷이(뭉개지거나 번지지 않게) · 무기·이펙트 없음 · 모든 칸 같은 크기 · 발바닥 높이 맞춤.

## ① 몸 시트 (머리 없음)  — 먼저 보낸다
첨부: 엄지 카포 전신 일러(옷 참고), 지난번 도트 몸 시트(칸 구성·느낌 참고).

```
Create a pixel-art battle sprite sheet of ONE character, BODY ONLY (no head).

PROPORTIONS (important)
- Stylized game-art proportions of about 4.5 heads tall: the head is clearly bigger than realistic proportions but the body is NOT a tiny chibi. Slim torso, medium-length legs, hands and feet slightly large and readable. Think of a popular pixel-art RPG hero, between a super-deformed chibi and a realistic character.
- The character is 100 pixels tall on a logical pixel grid (feet to the top of the magenta head circle), then scaled up by exactly 5x so every pixel is a crisp 5x5 block.

STYLE
- True pixel art: hard pixel edges, NO anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO gradients, NO dithering. Hand-painted pixel shading with 3-4 tone steps per material. At most 28 colors in the whole sheet. 1-pixel dark outline.
- Illustration-like rendering: keep the coat's gold trim, belt buckle, gold chain across the vest and the shoulder ornament readable as clear pixel clusters.
- Side view, facing RIGHT. Empty hands (fists). No weapon, no effects, no motion lines, no shadow, no text.
- Outfit (reference attached): black pinstripe three-piece suit with vest, white shirt, red tie, brown belt with gold buckle, black gloves, brown boots with a small heel, and a long dark-red overcoat with gold trim worn open and hanging behind the shoulders.

HEAD ANCHOR (very important)
- NO head. Where the head would be, draw ONE solid pure magenta circle (#FF00FF), flat color, no outline, diameter = 22 pixels (22% of the body height). It sits on the neck in every frame and moves with the body (leans back when hit, tilts when falling).

BACKGROUND: perfectly flat pure green #00FF00, nothing else.

LAYOUT (5 rows, one pose per row, frames left to right). Every cell the same size (170x170 logical pixels), the character's feet on the same bottom line (lying frames rest on that line), 8 pixels of margin between cells.
Row 1  IDLE (loop), 4 frames: gentle breathing, coat hem sways 1 pixel, body bobs 1 pixel.
Row 2  ATTACK, 5 frames: 1 ready crouch, 2 wind-up leaning back with the right arm pulled back, 3 lunge forward with the right arm fully extended, 4 follow-through, 5 recover to standing.
Row 3  DODGE, 3 frames: 1 start, 2 leaning back and stepping away with the coat flaring, 3 return.
Row 4  HIT, 3 frames: 1 flinch, 2 strongest recoil leaning far back, 3 recover.
Row 5  DOWN, 4 frames: 1 stagger, 2 knees buckle, 3 falling backwards, 4 lying flat (the magenta circle at the left end).
No labels, borders, grid lines, numbers or watermarks. Identical proportions and outfit in every frame.
```

## ② 머리 시트 (단발 · 숏컷)  — 몸이 마음에 들면 보낸다
첨부: 지난번 도트 머리 시트(느낌 참고) + ①의 몸 시트 한 장(크기 참고).

```
Create a pixel-art HEAD sprite sheet to be placed on the magenta circle of the attached body sheet.

STYLE: same as the attached body sheet (true pixel art, 5x scale, hard edges, no anti-aliasing, 1-pixel dark outline, flat pure green #00FF00 background, facing RIGHT). Each head is about 22 pixels wide at the same scale as the body. Illustration-like pixel shading with clean highlights.

COLOR RULES (the game recolors these):
- Hair: ONLY neutral gray shades (4 steps: dark, mid, light, highlight), no color tint.
- Eyes: ONLY one saturated cyan (#00C8FF) pixel group per eye, nothing else cyan.
- Skin pale peach. A small gold earring.

TWO HAIR SHAPES (two rows)
Row A BOB (chin-length bob, bangs over one eye).  Row B SHORT (short messy cut with one tuft sticking up).
EACH ROW has 5 poses in this order: 1 IDLE (calm, neutral mouth) · 2 ATTACK (determined eyes, mouth slightly open) · 3 DODGE (face turned slightly away, eyes narrowed) · 4 HIT (eyes shut, teeth clenched, head thrown back) · 5 DOWN (eyes closed, head tilted on its side).
Every cell the same size, the chin line at the same height in poses 1-4, no neck, nothing else in the image, no labels or borders.
```

## ③ 일러스트(상태창)는?
- 도트가 4.5등신이면 상태창 전신 일러도 같은 4.5등신 "일러 스타일"로 맞추는 것이 좋다(같은 캐릭터로 읽힌다).
- 도트 몸·머리 시트가 통과한 뒤, 같은 비율로 일러 몸 시트(마젠타 원)와 머리 시트를 새로 받는다. 지금 있는 치비 일러 시트는 그때까지 임시로 쓴다.
